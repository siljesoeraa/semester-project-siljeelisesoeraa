Education

Learn. Explore. Experiment.

At the museum, learning comes alive through hands-on experiments, interactive exhibitions, and the fascinating story of Fredrikstad's industrial heritage. Inspired by Fredrikstad Mekaniske Verksted, our educational programmes connect science, engineering, mathematics, and technology with local history and real-world problem-solving.

Pupils are encouraged to ask questions, test ideas, investigate how things work, and work together to find solutions. All programmes are curriculum-linked and combine guided activities with opportunities for experimentation, investigation, collaboration, and creative problem-solving. We offer three programmes adapted for different age groups and can also customise visits to suit your class's learning objectives. Contact us to find out more.

Why visit us?

A museum visit gives pupils the opportunity to take classroom learning into the real world. Through experiments, challenges, exploration, and collaboration, students develop their understanding of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics while discovering the industrial history of their local community.

Whether they are investigating why ships float, exploring engineering principles, solving practical challenges, or discovering how technology changed Fredrikstad, every visit encourages curiosity and shows how science and engineering can be used to understand the past and shape the future.

Years 1-4: Shipbuilders for a Day

Young learners discover the world of shipbuilding through play, storytelling, experiments, and practical activities. During their visit, pupils investigate why ships float, explore simple machines used in shipyards, and discover how people worked together to design and build ships at Fredrikstad Mekaniske Verksted.

Through hands-on activities, pupils are encouraged to ask questions, make predictions, test their ideas, and discover how science and engineering can help solve problems.

The focus of learning will be on:

  • Local history and community
  • Floating, sinking and buoyancy
  • Simple machines and engineering
  • Observation, experimentation, and problem-solving

Years 5-7: Engerneering the Industrial City

Pupils explore how Fredrikstad grew into an important industrial city and discover the engineering and technology behind its development. Through interactive exhibits, experiments, and practical challenges, they investigate ship construction, mechanical engineering, and the science behind buoyancy, forces, and stability.

Pupils are encouraged to investigate how things work, test ideas, use measurements, and apply engineering principles to solve practical problems.

The focus of learning will be on:

  • Industrial history and technological development
  • Engineering design and problem-solving
  • Forces, motion and buoyancy
  • Collaboration and critical thinking

Years 8-10: Innovation - From Shipyard to Sustainable Future

Older students explore how industrial innovation has transformed society from the nineteenth century to today. By connecting the history of Fredrikstad Mekaniske Verksted with modern engineering, technology, and sustainability, pupils investigate how scientific and technological advances have shaped industry and how innovation can help solve real-world challenges.

Through investigation, practical challenges, and design activities, pupils analyse problems, develop solutions, test ideas, and consider the environmental and social impact of technological development.

The focus of learning will be on:

  • Industrialisation, technology and social change
  • Maritime technology and engineering innovation
  • Energy, forces, and engineering principles
  • Mathematical modelling, calculations and design
